Agents are only as capable as the context they can reach. An agent with reliable access to the right information makes decisions grounded in fact; one without it guesses. And the work agents produce is itself a critical asset: every execution trace, intermediate result, and outcome is the raw material teams need to evaluate, debug, and improve them.
